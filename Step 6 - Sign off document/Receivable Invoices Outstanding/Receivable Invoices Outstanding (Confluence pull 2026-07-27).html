Part A. What exists today

1. What it is

Shows how much money is owed to the organisation in unpaid invoices, grouped by how overdue each invoice is, so staff can prioritise which outstanding amounts to chase. It is the dashboard's accounts-receivable aging view.

SME Receivable invoices are used a lot by those using Accounts Receivable (Marvin).

2. Where the data comes from

The Accounts Receivable module. Only posted invoices with an outstanding balance remaining after payments, discounts and write-offs are included; voided invoices are excluded. Each invoice is placed in an age bucket by days past its due date. Tables: ARInvoice (posted status, amounts, due dates), ARInvoiceDetail (line items). Prior doc, to confirm.

Part B. Users and market

5. Personas

Collections-centric. The bookkeeper / AR clerk (P1) is the primary user - working the overdue list to bring cash in. The treasurer / finance committee (P3) reads it as a cash-flow and working-capital signal. In HQ/denominational and school contexts, billing administrators (insurance billing, pension billing) and the school business office use the revenue-center/source filters to see their own receivables.

7. Context scenarios

A. Weekly collections triage - work the oldest buckets first. B. Chase the 90+/121+ balances (recovery drops after 90 days). C. HQ/school scopes to Insurance Billing or School to see their own receivables. D. Month-end AR review and cash-flow read. E. "Who are our worst debtors?" - hard today (bucket-first, no customer view). F. Nothing outstanding - should be a positive state, not a blank pie.

8. Competitor and analog benchmark

Desktop research The AR aging report is a standard collections/cash-flow triage tool. Consistent best practice: show buckets as an ordered bar (Current -> 121+) with green/amber/red severity, not a pie, because aging is inherently ordered and a pie loses the gradient and can't trend; pair it with KPI callouts (total outstanding, overdue % , DSO, "% over 60/90 days"); surface top overdue customers (customer concentration), not just bucket totals; and make every element prompt a next step ("click overdue -> collector task"), since the real gap is between reviewing the report and actually collecting. Sources: Resolut, Vertaccount, SW Recovery, ApprovalMax.

8b. SME. Used a lot by AR users; reinforces the SME's broader theme of actionable, aging widgets that link to a next step (see also My Status and Purchasing Management).

The concrete make-up, so it can be built and demoed consistently. A floor, not a straitjacket. Triage: P current production (keep what works, delete nothing used), C competitor, SME Ben Lane, DR desktop/best practice, O Oisin's demo.

  • Group by toggle: by aging bucket (today) or by customer (top debtors). [DR customer concentration]

  • Refresh with a "data as of" timestamp. [P pattern]

Views:

  • Glance (KPI): total outstanding, total overdue ($ and %), and DSO / "% over 60-90 days" if available. [DR]

  • Aging chart: an ordered bar (or single stacked bar) by bucket, Current -> 121+, with green/amber/red severity - replacing the pie. Live 23 Jul 2026 the whole balance sits in 121 & over, which a pie renders as one uninformative full circle - the ordered bar shows severity at a glance. [DR aging convention + verified live]

  • Bucket table + totals (keep). [P]

  • Top overdue customers - the actionable cut (who owes the most / worst overdue). [DR]

  • Detail drill (keep, move into a focus overlay): invoices in a bucket/for a customer, expandable to line items / attachments / notes / payments, with Export. [P]

Interaction & rules:

  • Make it prompt a next step: from a drilled invoice/customer, offer a collection action (open the invoice, record a follow-up) rather than a read-only list. [SME actionable; DR "click overdue -> task"]

  • Values as text, not pie-hover-only; name controls; keyboard operable. [accessibility]

  • Clarify the filter-to-chart coupling and whether a global dashboard filter applies. [prior-doc open questions]

11. Significant design decisions

11.1 Ordered bar, not a pie. Aging is an ordered severity gradient (Current -> 121+); an ordered/stacked bar with green-amber-red reads faster and can trend, where a pie cannot. Verified live 23 Jul 2026: the whole balance sits in 121 & over, so today's pie is a single full circle - exactly the case the ordered bar handles better. 11.2 Add a glance KPI - total outstanding, overdue $ and %, DSO if available - so the health read is immediate. 11.3 Add a customer/top-debtor view - bucket totals don't tell you who to call; customer concentration is the actionable cut. 11.4 Make it actionable - let a drilled invoice/customer prompt a collection next step, not just display; keep the rich invoice detail (line items/attachments/notes/payments/Export) inside a focus overlay. 11.5 Clarify filters - Revenue Center vs Source relationship, dynamic vs fixed lists, and global-filter response. 11.6 Accessibility parity - values as text, named controls.
