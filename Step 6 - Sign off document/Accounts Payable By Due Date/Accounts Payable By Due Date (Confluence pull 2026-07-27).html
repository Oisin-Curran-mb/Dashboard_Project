Part A. What exists today

1. What it is

Shows all outstanding unpaid supplier invoices grouped by due date - what needs paying and when. It is the dashboard's payables view: upcoming payment obligations at a glance.

SME Accounts Payable is a core module, but AP invoices are not used much today: clients accustomed to QuickBooks prefer a one-step check/pay process, and the pattern they actually want is an aging / actionable payables widget (Marvin). So this widget's redesign is as much about earning use as polishing what's there.

2. Where the data comes from

The Accounts Payable module. Only posted invoices with a due date and an outstanding balance are included; fully paid invoices are excluded. The due dates in the dropdown are the actual due dates of real invoices (dynamic, not user-configured). Tables: APInvoice (vendor, due date, posted/payment status), APInvoiceDetails (line items), APVendor. Prior doc, to confirm.

Part B. Users and market

5. Personas

Payables-centric. The bookkeeper / AP clerk (P1) is the primary user - deciding what to pay and when. The treasurer / finance committee (P3) reads it as a cash-requirements and working-capital signal. A pastor / executive may approve large payments. The SME's note is that many clients currently bypass AP invoicing and just write checks, so the widget must earn its use.

7. Context scenarios

A. Weekly "what's due this week and do we have the cash?". B. Payment run - select bills and pay. C. Overdue check - what's late, avoid fees. D. Cash planning for the month. E. The SME's reality - clients bypass AP invoicing for a one-step check, so the widget must be actionable to be used.

8. Competitor and analog benchmark

Desktop research The standard is the AP aging report: outstanding payables grouped into due-date buckets (current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+), used to prioritise payments, forecast cash requirements, avoid late fees and catch early-payment discounts. Best practice: an ordered, time-based view (buckets or a due-date timeline) rather than a pie; overdue emphasis; a cash-needed KPI; vendor rollup; and an actionable path to pay/schedule (Bill.com, Melio, Tipalti, NetSuite, QuickBooks bill pay). Reviewed weekly, before each payment run, and at close. Sources: Yooz, NetSuite, Invoice Data Extraction, GEP.

8b. SME. Core module, but AP invoices underused because clients prefer a one-step check; the desired pattern is an aging/action widget. This is the strongest "make it actionable" signal of any widget.

Part C. Designer brief

9. Target composition (what the widget should be built from)

The concrete make-up, so it can be built and demoed consistently. A floor, not a straitjacket. Triage: P current production (keep what works, delete nothing used), C competitor, SME Ben Lane, DR desktop/best practice, O Oisin's demo.

Controls (header):

  • Time frame / grouping - group by due-date buckets (overdue / due this week / this month / later) rather than a raw single-date dropdown; keep a specific-date option. And sync the chart to the selection (see 11.4). [P selector verified live 23 Jul 2026; DR aging buckets]

  • Optional vendor filter. Refresh with a "data as of" timestamp. [P]

Views:

  • Glance (KPI): total payable, overdue amount, and cash needed this week/month. [DR cash requirements]

  • Due-date chart: a date-ordered bar / timeline of amounts due over time (a cash-outflow calendar), labelled by date, with overdue emphasised - replacing the amount-labelled pie. [DR; fixes the P pie verified live 23 Jul 2026]

  • Table: vendor, invoice number, due date, amount, days-until/overdue; totals. Fix the £ hard-coded currency to follow the org. [P (Vendor | Inv. # | Amount verified live 23 Jul 2026) + aging]

  • Vendor rollup (top owed) as an optional cut. [DR]

Interaction & rules:

  • Make it payable-actionable: select invoices and pay / schedule payment / create a check from the widget, following the Payroll STO inline-action pattern, or link straight into the AP payment workflow. This is the single biggest reason to use it. [SME]

  • Sync chart and table - the chart should reflect the current filter (or act as the selector); resolve the table-only coupling, per the good Pension Plans model. [P chart-not-synced confirmed by interaction test 23 Jul 2026]

  • Pair with available cash (Bank Balances) for a "can we cover it?" read. [cross-widget]

  • Values as text; name controls; keyboard; fix the £ localisation defect. [accessibility]

10. States and constraints

Item

What it means

Nothing due / nothing overdue

A positive "nothing due" state, not a blank pie.

No AP module / no rights

Hidden or explicit no-access. Confirm entitlement. @Oisin Curran

Loading / error

Loading + retry; never a blank chart that reads as "nothing owed".

Posted-with-balance basis

Only posted invoices with a due date and remaining balance; paid excluded. Confirm aging basis is due date (not invoice date). Label it.

Localisation / accessibility (Pathway AA)

Currency follows the org; values as text; named controls. Verified live 23 Jul 2026 currency renders as £ (GBP) regardless of org - localisation defect to fix.

Cross-widget consistency

The filter-affects-table-only + all-dates-chart pattern is shared with Deposit Accounts and Loans With Balance Due - fix it consistently across all three (11.4).

11. Significant design decisions

11.1 Reframe as payables aging + cash requirements - not a flat due-date list: lead with overdue and "cash needed soon". 11.2 Make it actionable (pay/schedule/one-step check, Payroll STO inline-action pattern) - the SME's core reason AP invoices are underused; the highest-value change here. 11.3 Date-ordered chart, not an amount-labelled pie - a timeline/bar of cash out over time, labelled by date, overdue emphasised (pie verified live 23 Jul 2026). 11.4 Sync chart and table, and fix it consistently across AP / Deposit Accounts / Loans (the shared table-only antipattern, confirmed not synced by interaction test 23 Jul 2026). 11.5 Glance KPI - total, overdue, cash-needed. 11.6 Pair with available cash (Bank Balances) for coverage. 11.7 Accessibility parity - values as text, named controls, fix the £ localisation defect.
