Part A. What exists today

1. What it is

Gives an overview of how much is being contributed annually across each pension plan type, filterable by church district, with a drill into which individual appointees (clergy) are on each plan. It is a denominational pension-administration widget: the conference's annual clergy-pension obligation, by plan.

SME HQ / denominational, module-dependent; used specifically by the Methodist-conference segment (roughly the same ~5% of clients as insurance billing), low traffic but important to those who use it (Marvin).

2. Where the data comes from

The Pension Billing area. It counts active pension plan appointments only (person active, plan started, not yet ended), summing annual contribution amounts grouped by plan type. Districts come from the same module. Tables: PBAppointmentPlan (appointments, start/end, annual amounts), PBControlTable (districts). Prior doc, to confirm. Context Plan types are named programs (e.g. CRSP, CPP, UMPIP); conferences are the plan sponsors and churches are billed monthly (Wespath-administered).

Part B. Users and market

5. Personas

Denominational benefits administration. The conference / denominational pension & benefits administrator is the primary user - managing clergy pension obligations across districts. The conference treasurer / finance reads the total obligation and funding. A church treasurer cares about their own monthly pension bill. HQ-specific (e.g. Methodist conference) audience.

8. Competitor and analog benchmark

Desktop research Denominational clergy-pension administration is niche (Wespath for the UMC; conferences are plan sponsors, churches billed monthly for named programs like CRSP/CPP/UMPIP). The relevant design pattern is a categorical cost breakdown with drill: for comparing amounts across a handful of plan types a horizontal bar reads more precisely than a pie (a pie is only comfortable with a few segments and one dominant share); pair with counts and totals, and drill to the members behind each category. Sources: UM News (Wespath, conferences as sponsors), NYAC (plan types, monthly billing), NTCUMC (CRSP).

Part C. Designer brief

9. Target composition (what the widget should be built from)

The concrete make-up, so it can be built and demoed consistently. A floor, not a straitjacket. Triage: P current production (keep what works, delete nothing used), C competitor, SME Ben Lane, DR desktop/best practice, O Oisin's demo.

Controls (header):

  • District dropdown (All Districts default; All Districts / District 1 / 2 / 3), syncing both views. Refresh with a "data as of" timestamp. [P - verified live 23 Jul 2026]

  • Export to Excel - fix the mismatch: make the export match the current view, or clearly offer both summary and appointee-detail exports. [P quirk]

  • Optional chart-type toggle (bar/pie) - resolves the open question. [P open question]

Views:

  • Glance (KPI): total annual contribution, number of active appointees, number of plan types. [DR]

  • Breakdown: a table (plan name, annual amount, appointee count, total) synced with a horizontal bar by plan type (clearer than the pie for comparison; keep pie/donut only if few types with one dominant). [P + DR]

  • Drill (preserve): plan -> appointees (name, church/org, annual amount), filter carried through, Export - move into a focus overlay. [P strength]

Interaction & rules:

  • Preserve the good behaviours - synced table+chart and drill-with-filter-carry-through; hold this widget up as the model for the widgets that don't sync (AP / Deposit Accounts / Loans). [P - table+chart sync verified live 23 Jul 2026]

  • Add appointee counts alongside amounts for context. [DR]

  • Values as text; named controls; keyboard. [accessibility]

  • Optionally connect the obligation to billing/payment status (is it being paid) - cross-widget with remittance/billing. [DR]

11. Significant design decisions

11.1 Preserve the good behaviours - synced table+chart and drill-with-filter-carry-through are exactly right; make this the reference pattern for the non-syncing widgets. 11.2 Bar over pie for the plan-type breakdown (precise comparison); resolve the pie-vs-bar open question, ideally with a toggle. 11.3 Add a glance KPI and appointee counts - amounts alone lack context. 11.4 Fix the export mismatch - summary vs appointee detail. 11.5 Accessibility parity - values as text, named controls. 11.6 (Optional) connect to billing/payment - whether the obligation is being paid, consistent with the remittance/pacing theme.
