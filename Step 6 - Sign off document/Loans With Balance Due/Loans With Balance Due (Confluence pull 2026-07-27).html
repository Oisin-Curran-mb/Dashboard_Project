Part A. What exists today

1. What it is

Shows all loans that currently have an outstanding balance and how overdue each is - who owes money, when they last paid, and how the loan book is ageing. It is loan-receivables aging: the org here is the lender (e.g. a denominational/foundation or extension fund lending to churches).

SME A low-usage, module-dependent widget (Loan Processing); relevant to the HQ/foundation lending segment (Marvin).

2. Where the data comes from

The Loan Processing module. It shows loans with at least one invoice and a remaining balance; amount due = principal + interest on invoices minus posted payments. Loan types are organisation-defined (not a fixed list). Tables: LNLoan (loans), LNInvoice (invoices), LNTypeRepository (loan types). Prior doc, to confirm.

Part B. Users and market

5. Personas

Lender-side. The loan administrator / servicer is the primary user - watching balances and chasing overdue loans. The treasurer / finance (P3) reads the loan book as a receivable and credit-risk exposure. In HQ/foundation contexts, extension-fund / loan-fund staff manage loans made to churches. Low-usage, HQ-leaning audience.

7. Context scenarios

A. Loan servicer works the overdue loans (oldest first). B. Finance reads portfolio risk (how much is 90+). C. Contact a borrower who has not paid recently (last-payment date). D. Filter to a loan type. E. Month-end loan-book review.

8. Competitor and analog benchmark

Desktop research Loan-servicing/portfolio dashboards read delinquency by days-past-due buckets (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 60/90+), track PAR (Portfolio at Risk) and delinquency rate by bucket, and drive collections (worst loans first; recovery drops after 90 days). Standard elements: an aging report (ordered, not a pie), outstanding by bucket, last-payment/dormancy signals, and a drill to the loan with collection notes/next-steps. Sources: Vergent (DPD buckets), Allied (bucket rates, roll rates), Lendsqr (PAR), Bryt.

9. Target composition (what the widget should be built from)

The concrete make-up, so it can be built and demoed consistently. A floor, not a straitjacket. Triage: P current production (keep what works, delete nothing used), C competitor, SME Ben Lane, DR desktop/best practice, O Oisin's demo.

Views:

  • Glance (KPI): total outstanding, total past-due, number of loans, and a portfolio-risk read (% past due / 90+ amount / PAR). [DR]

  • Aging chart: an ordered bar by delinquency bucket with accurate labels (Current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 90+), green-amber-red - replacing the current pie (pie confirmed live 23 Jul 2026). [DR; fixes P labels + pie]

Interaction & rules:

  • Collections-actionable: clarify where the account link goes (open question) and make it a collections next-step (open the loan / borrower / record contact). [P link; DR]

  • Flag dormancy - long time since last payment. [DR early-warning]

  • Values as text; named controls; keyboard. [accessibility]

10. States and constraints

Amount-due basis

Principal + interest on invoices minus posted payments; loans need at least one invoice + a balance. Label it.

Cross-widget consistency

Filter-affects-table-only + all-loans-chart is shared with Deposit Accounts and AP - fix consistently (11.3).

11. Significant design decisions

11.1 Fix the aging labels - accurate day ranges (Current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 90+); the current "60"/"Over 60" labels mislead. 11.2 Ordered bar, not a pie - delinquency is an ordered severity gradient; expose values as text. 11.3 Sync chart to filter, consistently across Loans / Deposit Accounts / AP (use Pension Plans' synced behaviour as the model). 11.4 Add a portfolio-risk KPI - total/past-due/90+/PAR. 11.5 Make it collections-actionable - clarify the account link and turn it into a next-step; flag dormancy via last-payment date. 11.6 Accessibility parity.
