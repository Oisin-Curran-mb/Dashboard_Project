Part A. What exists today

1. What it is

Shows how gift pledges are tracking against what has been received, for each pledge purpose, as of a chosen date. It is the dashboard's pledge-campaign / stewardship tracker: how a building fund, capital campaign or annual pledge drive is progressing.

SME A module-dependent widget for HQ / nonprofit organisations (Donors and Gifts); lower traffic than the core accounting widgets, but central to the fundraising audience that uses it (Marvin).

2. Where the data comes from

The Donors and Gifts module. A "Date Gifts Thru" thru-date filter (default = today, UK dd/mm/yyyy) controls which gifts are counted - only gifts received on or before it are included - and a committed (Refreshed) date persists per user/widget across reload. Verified live 23 Jul 2026. Table: GFPledge (pledge records by purpose, with totals and received amounts) - prior doc, to confirm.

Part B. Users and market

5. Personas

Stewardship/fundraising-centric. Development / stewardship staff are the primary users - managing a campaign and following up on unfulfilled pledges. The bookkeeper / finance (P1) records pledges and forecasts revenue. Pastor / leadership and the board read campaign progress; the treasurer (P3) watches expected income. This is an HQ/nonprofit and church-stewardship audience.

7. Context scenarios

A. Stewardship staff check how the building-fund campaign is progressing. B. Board report - progress toward the goal. C. "Who is behind on their pledge?" - follow-up (can't drill to donors today). D. As-of-date reporting for a finance report. E. Year-end pledge status and expected carry-forward.

8. Competitor and analog benchmark

Desktop research Pledge/campaign tracking in donor CRMs and church giving tools (Blackbaud, DonorPerfect, Bloomerang, Planning Center Giving, Donorbox, Keela). The standard reads: progress toward goal (a per-campaign progress bar / thermometer, and overall), pledged vs received in real time for board/stakeholder reporting, a fulfillment percentage (received / pledged; nonprofits typically 85-95%, assume 8-10% won't materialise), a pledge aging view of overdue installments (30/60/90) to drive donor follow-up, and donor-level outstanding-balance tracking with reminders. Sources: DonorSnap, DonorPerfect, RallyUp (PFP), Planning Center.

Part C. Designer brief

9. Target composition (what the widget should be built from)

The concrete make-up, so it can be built and demoed consistently. A floor, not a straitjacket. Triage: P current production (keep what works, delete nothing used), C competitor, SME Ben Lane, DR desktop/best practice, O Oisin's demo.

Controls (header):

  • Gifts received through (relabel "Date Gifts Thru") thru-date picker, default today, UK dd/mm/yyyy; committed date persists across reload (verified live 23 Jul 2026). Refresh anchor with a "data as of" timestamp. [P verified, relabelled]

  • Optional purpose/campaign filter. [DR]

Views:

  • Glance (KPI): total pledged, received, % fulfilled, outstanding - across purposes. [DR]

  • Progress per purpose: a progress bar / thermometer showing received vs pledge total, with a marker for "due by now", plus an overall goal bar. [DR fundraising standard]

  • Table: Purpose, Pledge Total, Pledge Due (by date), Received, Due Remaining, % Fulfilled (reframe from Percent Due) + totals. [P verified, relabelled]

  • Behind-schedule cut: purposes/pledges that are behind (Due Remaining > 0), leading to donor follow-up. [DR pledge aging]

Interaction & rules:

  • Drill a purpose -> the donors/pledges behind for follow-up, in a focus overlay. [DR stewardship; today none]

  • Reframe % Due -> % fulfilled (progress), outstanding as the complement; show ahead-of-schedule (over-received) positively. [DR]

  • Make it board/report-ready (it is a stakeholder-reporting widget). [DR]

  • Values as text; named controls; keyboard. [accessibility]

11. Significant design decisions

11.1 Add a progress visual. Progress toward goal (per-purpose bar/thermometer + overall) is the campaign-standard read; a table alone hides it. 11.2 Lead with fulfillment. Show % fulfilled (received/pledged) as the headline; keep due/remaining for the time-phased view; reframe "% Due" and show ahead-of-schedule positively. 11.3 Glance KPI - total pledged/received/% fulfilled/outstanding. 11.4 Add donor follow-up drill - which pledges/donors are behind (pledge aging) so stewardship can act. 11.5 Board-ready - this is a reporting widget; make the progress read presentation-quality. 11.6 Sibling consistency with Remittance Pledges (date persistence confirmed aligned 23 Jul 2026). 11.7 Accessibility parity - keep values as text.
